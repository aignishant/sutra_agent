## 1. Non-negotiables that get broken most often

- **No clocks anywhere.** Not "20 minutes", not "should take about an hour", not "a quick detour".
  `./m depth` greps for them and fails the day.
- **The story section carries no jargon at all**, and must be a scene the reader has *plausibly
  lived in* — a courier and a parcel, a repair-shop job card, a shared water connection, a bus
  route, a used car checked by a mechanic, a landlord reading a meter, a queue at a counter.
  **Not** a nautical chart, a model railway, a theatre programme, a projection booth, a historical
  disaster, an orchestra, a lighthouse. The scene must hold the actual failure the part teaches,
  and **no two parts in one day may use the same metaphor family** — grep your own day before
  choosing.
- **`In production` is mandatory on every part** and must contain: what a professional writes
  instead of the teaching version, what degrades at scale or under concurrency, **the review
  comment a senior engineer leaves** (quoted), and **the interview question** with an honest
  spoken answer.
- **Every code fence that teaches is followed immediately by a `**Line by line:**` list.**
- **`TODO(me)` stays unsolved.** You teach; you never do the learner's reps.
- **No person names, no course/creator/company-brand names.** Tool names (ADK, MCP, Gemini, Groq,
  uv, ruff, SQLite, Postgres) are required and fine.
- **Never invent an API, a version, a model string or a citation.** See §5 below.
- **Write only your day folder.** Never touch `sutra/`, `sutra_mcp/`, `skills/`, `tests/`,
  `pyproject.toml`, `docs/`, or any other day. The learner types every line of project code.
- **Second person, present tense, active voice.** Grammar and punctuation are part of the
  deliverable; the reader reads in a second language. No run-on sentences, no long em-dash chains.

## 2. The mechanical contract (what `./m depth <N>` actually checks)

**Folder shape** — `days/day-NN-<slug>/` with `LESSON.md`, `CHECKLIST.md`, `parts/`, optionally

are `parts/NN-<slug>/<section>.<sub>-<slug>.md`. The folder number and the number before the dot

## 3. How to split the day

- One section per curriculum ID, plus a **synthesis** section where the IDs meet, plus an
  **in-production** section. Most days land at 5–7 sections and **14–20 parts**.
- **At least one part whose subject is a deliberate failure** — the legacy day's failure lab,
  promoted to a part of its own, usually `production`.
- The day climbs `foundation → working → production`.
- One idea per part. If it needs "also" to introduce its second half, it is two parts.
- A part runs roughly 200–320 lines. Depth comes from explanation, never from padding.
- Every part is readable cold: name and **link** its prerequisite part, including parts from
  earlier days, by relative path — for example
  `../../../day-26-loading-skills-into-adk/parts/01-loading-a-folder/1.3-building-a-skill-in-code.md`.

- **API.** Every ADK or MCP SDK symbol you use is verified against the live docs **on the day you
  write**, and the part says so inline: *"Verified against `https://adk.dev/docs/…` on 2026-09-04."*
  Use WebFetch. If a symbol cannot be confirmed, write the code the plan implies and leave
  `TODO(verify: <exact command or URL>)` beside it — never a confident invention.
- **Version.** Look it up live with
  `curl -s https://pypi.org/pypi/<pkg>/json` and read `info.version`. Already pinned in
  `pyproject.toml`: `google-adk==2.7.1`, `google-genai==2.19.0`, `mcp==1.29.1`, `ruff==0.16.4`,
  `pytest==9.1.1`. **Do not add a package unless your day genuinely installs one**; if it does,
  the hub's §11 carries the `docs/PACKAGES.md` row for the orchestrator to append.
- **Model.** Free tier only (Addendum 02): Gemini Flash-class via `GOOGLE_API_KEY` with
  `GOOGLE_GENAI_USE_VERTEXAI=FALSE`, Groq, OpenRouter `:free`, or local Ollama. Every call path
  handles HTTP 429 with `retry-after` and backoff. `openrouter/` strings must end `:free`.
  Reuse the exact model string earlier days already use — grep `days/` for it rather than
  inventing one.
- **Citation.** Only cite a paper whose row already exists in `docs/PAPERS.md`, **or** one the
  orchestrator handed you in your task prompt as verified. **Do not invent an arXiv ID or a DOI,
  and do not add a paper part unless your task prompt gives you a verified citation.** A day with

  §6 *The paper behind it* is an **address only**: the citation block, one sentence of the claim,
